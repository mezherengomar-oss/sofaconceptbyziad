SofaConcept

Where craftsmanship meets timeless elegance

Sofa Concept, founded by Ziad Mezher, is born from a passion for fine living. We design and create furniture that blends comfort, sophistication and lasting quality, transforming every space into a refined sanctuary.

Each piece is shaped with meticulous attention to detail: premium materials, precise tailoring and a design language that stays graceful through the years. From statement sofas to elegant accent pieces, our collection is made for those who appreciate the art of beautiful interiors.

Whether you are furnishing a private residence, a villa or a boutique hospitality space, we are here to guide you to the piece that feels truly yours.
